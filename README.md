# Viral Score Lab

A free, single-page tool that scores a short-form post for viral potential before you publish it, and diagnoses its stats after.

Works for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, X and LinkedIn.

## What it does

**Before posting:** paste the hook, on-screen text, script, caption and hashtags. It scores the post out of 100 as you type, across:

- Hook (30)
- Retention (20)
- Caption & search (20)
- Hashtags (10)
- Share value (20)

It also lists the top things to fix first.

**After posting:** enter views, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows, followers and your usual views. It works out share rate, save rate, engagement, comment rate, follows per 1,000 views, reach beyond your followers, and how many times your usual views the post got.

## Notes

- The score uses fixed rules based on common short-form advice. It's a checklist, not a prediction.
- The "Deep analysis with Claude" and "Diagnose with Claude" buttons only appear when the page is opened as a Claude artifact. On GitHub Pages they're hidden and the rest of the tool works offline.
- History is stored in your browser only.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no dependencies.
