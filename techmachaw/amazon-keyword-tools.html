Keyword Processor

Paste messy keyword lists from anywhere. It removes duplicates, brand names and filler words, and packs the rest into a backend search terms line under Amazon's 250 byte limit.

Words like best, cheap, sale and prices are left out of the backend line automatically, because Amazon does not allow claims there. Check the word list for other brands' names and add them above: using a competitor's brand in your search terms breaks Amazon's rules.
